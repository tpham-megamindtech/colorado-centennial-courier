Denver International Airport reported record annual passenger traffic this past year, continuing a growth trajectory that airport officials say reinforces the facility's role as a major economic driver for the broader Denver metro region.

Airport officials attribute the growth to a combination of expanded domestic route offerings, new international service additions, and continued strong leisure and business travel demand tied to the region's overall economic growth. "Every additional route is also additional jobs and additional spending in the surrounding area," said airport spokesperson Regina Aldous.

## Economic Impact Extends Beyond the Terminal

Economic impact studies commissioned by the airport estimate that its operations, along with related businesses in cargo, hospitality, and logistics located near the facility, support tens of thousands of regional jobs. Airport officials point to continued warehouse and logistics facility development near the airport as evidence of this broader economic ripple effect.

The airport has continued to invest in terminal expansion and infrastructure upgrades to accommodate passenger growth, with several capital improvement projects currently underway aimed at increasing capacity ahead of continued anticipated traffic growth.

## Balancing Growth With Community Concerns

Not all reaction to the airport's growth has been uniformly positive, with some nearby communities raising concerns about noise and traffic congestion tied to increased flight volume and airport-adjacent development. Airport officials say they've expanded community engagement efforts and noise mitigation programs in response to these concerns.

Regional economic development officials say the airport's continued growth remains a key asset in attracting new business investment to the metro area, citing air service connectivity as a factor cited by several companies that have chosen to relocate or expand operations in the region recently.
