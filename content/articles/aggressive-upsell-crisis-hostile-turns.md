A customer reviewing her experiences at **Jasmine Nails & Spa** described how a relationship that began with positive visits gradually deteriorated into frustration. According to the review, what started as a promising salon ultimately lost her trust because of perceived sales pressure, inconsistent service quality, and an unsatisfactory customer experience.

## From Loyal Customer to Feeling Unwelcome

The customer explained that she began visiting the salon in August and returned approximately once each month. She also introduced her boyfriend to the business, hoping they could become regular clients together.

She said the first few visits went well, although service quality began declining several months later. At first, the couple chose to overlook those experiences, believing they may have simply reflected an off day.

The turning point came during a November appointment.

## Pressure to Upgrade

According to the customer, both she and her boyfriend selected the **$60 pedicure**. She said the technicians encouraged them to upgrade to the **$75 package**. After they politely declined, she felt the atmosphere changed noticeably and believed the staff became less welcoming.

## Concerns About Service Quality

The customer reported that both she and her boyfriend experienced cuts around their cuticles. She said one cut continued bleeding well after the visit and that rubbing alcohol was applied, but she did not feel her concerns were acknowledged appropriately.

The couple ultimately spent nearly **$300 including tips** during the visit, yet left disappointed by both the service and the overall experience.

## Editorial Perspective

Upselling can be appropriate when presented as an option rather than an expectation. Customers who feel pressured after declining an upgrade may question whether recommendations are intended to benefit them or simply increase the bill.

When accidental cuts occur, prompt empathy, clear communication, and professional follow-up often play a significant role in preserving customer trust.

## Key Takeaways

- Optional upgrades should remain optional.
- Respecting customer decisions encourages loyalty.
- Service-related injuries deserve prompt and empathetic responses.
- Consistency is essential for retaining repeat customers.
- Long-term relationships are often more valuable than short-term sales.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this review illustrates how perceived sales pressure and inconsistent service can outweigh an otherwise loyal customer relationship. Consistent professionalism and respectful communication remain essential for sustainable growth.
