A bride-to-be reviewing her experience at **Noire The Nail Bar** described an appointment that she said left her dealing with injuries, damaged nails, and disappointment just days before her wedding. According to her account, the experience raised concerns not only about technical execution but also about how customer issues were handled after the service.

## Injuries During the Appointment

The customer said both of her big toes were accidentally cut during the pedicure, leaving them bleeding. She also reported that one fingernail broke completely only two days later, shortly before her wedding.

## Paying Full Price

According to the review, the total bill was approximately **$170**. The customer said she paid the full amount despite the injuries and did not receive any discount or compensation.

## No Follow-Up

The customer further stated that after reporting the problems, she did not receive meaningful follow-up before her wedding weekend and felt her concerns were left unresolved.

## Editorial Perspective

Appointments tied to weddings carry especially high expectations. When unexpected issues occur, prompt communication, empathy, and reasonable efforts to resolve concerns can help preserve customer trust. Even when mistakes happen, thoughtful follow-up demonstrates professionalism and accountability.

## Key Takeaways

- Careful technique helps reduce accidental injuries.
- Prompt communication is important after service concerns.
- Post-service follow-up can strengthen customer confidence.
- Wedding appointments require extra attention to quality.
- Professional service recovery supports long-term reputation.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this review highlights how technical quality and responsive customer care together shape the overall client experience.
