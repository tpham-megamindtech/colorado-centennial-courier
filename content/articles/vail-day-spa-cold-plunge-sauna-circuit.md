Alpine Bath House has added a full contrast therapy circuit to its Vail Village location, giving skiers and hotel guests access to a cold plunge pool, traditional Finnish sauna, and steam room built specifically for post-activity recovery.

General manager Erik Solheim says the circuit was designed after tracking a rise in requests for cold plunge access, a trend he attributes partly to broader interest in recovery science and partly to the property's ski-in clientele looking for something more targeted than a standard hot tub soak. "Guests were already asking for cold plunge specifically, by name," he said. "That's relatively new for us."

## Building a Circuit, Not Just a Pool

Rather than adding a single cold plunge tub, the property built a full rotation: sauna, cold plunge, rest, and repeat, with staff on hand to guide first-time users through recommended timing. Solheim says the guided format has been important, since several early guests initially treated the cold plunge as a quick dip rather than following the contrast therapy protocol the circuit is designed around.

The property has priced circuit access as a standalone day pass, separate from its massage and facial menu, a structure Solheim says allows guests to book a recovery session without committing to a longer spa treatment. Day pass sales have grown steadily since the circuit's debut this ski season, with weekend afternoon slots now booking out regularly.

## Following a Broader Recovery Trend

Cold plunge and contrast therapy offerings have expanded across several Colorado mountain towns over the past two winters, mirroring a broader wellness industry trend toward recovery-focused treatments over purely relaxation-focused ones. Solheim says he expects the format to become a standard offering at mountain spas within a few years, similar to how infrared saunas became common a decade ago.

Alpine Bath House is now considering adding a summer trail-running recovery package built around the same circuit, aimed at capturing warm-weather visitors once ski season ends.
