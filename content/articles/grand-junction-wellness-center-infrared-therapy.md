Mesa Vitality Wellness Center has added four private infrared sauna suites to its Grand Junction location, expanding a recovery-focused service category that owner Bret Halloway says has been underserved on the Western Slope.

"Most of what people think of as recovery services — infrared sauna, cryotherapy, that kind of thing — has been concentrated on the Front Range," Halloway said. "There wasn't a dedicated option out here, so people either drove hours or went without."

## Filling a Regional Service Gap

Grand Junction's wellness market has historically leaned toward traditional massage and chiropractic services, and Halloway says the addition of infrared suites required building an entirely new client education process, since many local clients had limited familiarity with the treatment before it became available nearby. The center has produced simple informational materials explaining what infrared therapy involves and who it's typically recommended for, material Halloway says has been necessary to convert curiosity into bookings.

Early demand has skewed toward clients managing chronic pain conditions and local endurance athletes training in the nearby Colorado National Monument trail system, a client mix Halloway says has shaped how the center markets the new suites.

## Building Toward a Broader Recovery Menu

The infrared suites are the first phase of a broader recovery services expansion Halloway has planned, with a cold plunge addition expected later this year pending equipment delivery. He says the center's long-term goal is to position itself as the primary recovery-focused wellness option for the broader Grand Junction and Western Slope region, rather than competing directly with Front Range wellness chains on their own terms.

Membership packages bundling infrared sessions with existing massage services have already outperformed initial projections, according to Halloway, who says the center is now considering extending hours to accommodate demand from shift workers at nearby healthcare facilities.
